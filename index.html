What Rewired does

You choose the apps that pull you in. Rewired blocks them with Apple's Screen Time and lets you earn time back with Brain Tokens — collected through real-world habits like walking, journaling, deep breathing and gratitude.

Common questions

Rewired asks for Screen Time access. Why?

Blocking apps on iPhone is only possible through Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls). Apple shows us only opaque tokens for the apps you pick — we never see which apps they are or how you use them. That data stays on your phone.

Apps are not being blocked

Check that Screen Time access is still on: Settings → Screen Time → Apps with Screen Time Access → Rewired. If it is on, open Rewired and select the apps again. Restarting the phone fixes most remaining cases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are handled by Apple, not by us. Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and cancel there. Cancelling at least 24 hours before the renewal date stops the next charge. Deleting the app does not cancel it.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I changed iPhone and my subscription is gone

Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap Restore purchases in the app.

How do I delete my data?

Deleting the app removes everything it kept on the device. If you have an account, email info@zintex.dev from its address and we will delete it.
