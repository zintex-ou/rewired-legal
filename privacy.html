Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Rewired is published by Zintex OÜ, a company registered in Estonia ("we"). This policy explains what the Rewired iPhone app does with your information.

The short version. Which apps you block and how you use your phone stay on your iPhone — Apple's Screen Time keeps them from us. We collect only what we need to run the app, sell subscriptions and understand how the app is used. We never sell your data.

Screen Time data

Rewired uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. Apple gives the app only anonymous tokens for the apps and websites you select. We cannot see their names, your usage or your browsing, and none of it leaves your device.

Habit data

Habits you log to earn Brain Tokens — walks, journal entries, breathing sessions, gratitude notes — are stored on your device. If you allow access to motion or step data, it is read on the device only to count your walks.

What we do collect

  • Account data, only if you create an account — email or Sign in with Apple identifier, stored with our backend provider.
  • Subscription status from Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Usage analytics — anonymous events such as screens opened and features used, to improve the app.
  • Install attribution — which ad campaign, if any, led to the install.
  • Technical data — device model, iOS and app version, crash logs.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase — backend and accounts.
  • Superwall — subscription screens.
  • PostHog — product analytics.
  • Appstack — install attribution.
  • Vercel — web hosting.
  • Apple — app distribution and subscription payments.

Some providers may process data outside the EEA under Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data.

How long we keep it

While you use the app or keep an account. We delete it when you ask, or after 24 months of inactivity, unless the law requires us to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction or a copy of your data, or object to processing, at info@zintex.dev. You can also complain to your data protection authority.

Children

Rewired is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
